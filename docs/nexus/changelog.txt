0.7.1 (2026-11-05)

- Doors, levers and chests open again (0.7.0 took the press and did nothing). The ERVR.ini workaround Pass2ActionButtons=true is now built in and the line is no longer read
- A strike is one swing: it ends when the swing slows (1.5 s at the latest) and the next needs a new swing, with a short cooldown after a hit. No more constant attacking, walls hit over and over, or people hit by a sword that only touched them
- Rolls and jumps cost stamina again during a strike, and a shield guards only when you raise it in front of your chest (an arm at rest held the guard, and stamina comes back slowly while guarding)
- Flat, third person with no head, after the Grafted Scion: when the game's loading signal stays on while you walk around, ERVR treats it as stuck and comes back
- Both stick clicks together are the menu button (tap: menu, hold: map), for SteamVR, which keeps a Quest's left menu button for its dashboard
- After a death, the left stick moves the Stake of Marika / last site of grace choice
- Test switch for a lift that stays put after its lever: [VR] Pass2EventsOnce=true in ERVR.ini

0.7.0 (2026-11-04)

- ERVR Settings in the game's own menu (System, to the right of Graphics; patch 1.17.2): walking, turning, physical melee, damage, weapon weight and angle, body calibration, world scale, the HUD and rumble, changed without leaving the headset

- Launcher Options > Combat > Swing threshold: how fast the blade must move to strike

0.6.0 (2026-11-02) - first public release

- ERVR Launcher: unpack anywhere, press START VR. It finds the game, checks its patch, the VR runtime and the headset, installs ERVR, parks flat render mods, turns off depth of field, motion blur and ray tracing, and starts the game with EAC off or through Seamless Co-op
- Seamless Co-op: partners play the ordinary game; your hits count only your own damage; deaths, joins and leaves no longer crash or break the view
- Game patch 1.17.2 (exe 2.7.2.0) next to 1.16.2 and 1.17.1
- Weapon wheel: hold a grip with the hand still, let go over a weapon to switch, with the game's own icons
- Body haptics on bHaptics TactSuit, TactSleeves and TactVisor through the bHaptics Player
- Combat: one hit per swing scaled by its quality; swings no longer stop registering after a spent swing
- Frame rate follows the headset (no more half-rate 45 Hz under Virtual Desktop); exact 16:9 render sizes keep menus and the map on the HUD panel
- Fixes: view near the ground or stuck crouched, black headset at the title screen, a pickup dialog that ignored every button, a long-session co-op crash
