0.8.0 (2026-11-12)

- Weapon Workshop: the launcher's Weapons tab gives any weapon, or a whole weapon type, its own VR profile - how it sits in your hand, how it swings, what your swings do. Every weapon and shield is read from your own game files with its real icon, DLC included. Share a profile as a code; imports are reviewed before they are saved, and nothing is uploaded anywhere
- Smoother: the headset gets a new frame every refresh. A pair of eyes a millisecond late used to drop the game to half rate (45-55 pairs a second at 90 Hz); now 75-83, the headset steady at 90
- Your Ash of War on the right trigger, held, one-handed too: a stance waits for your swing and that swing is its follow-up; a charged skill lets go when you do. A sword's skill projectile leaves from your blade and flies where you swung, Moonveil's wave in the plane of the swing; Reduvia's blood blades fly where your hand points
- Backstabs: swing at an enemy's back, close and facing it, and the game plays its backstab
- Dodges roll the way the stick points (every roll went where you looked)
- Site of grace and NPC menus move with the left stick, and your swings and the right stick stay quiet behind them
- Torrent: your body stays in the saddle at a gallop and in jumps instead of popping up in front of you; turning your body no longer rotates the horse 1:1 - look around freely, turn past 45 degrees and Torrent turns that way when you push the stick
- Linux (Proton): full stereo starts (it stayed on the flat screen)
- A crash at boot with Seamless Co-op is gone

0.7.5 (2026-11-07)

- Interactions fixed at the root: ERVR draws the right eye by running the game's frame a second time, and every interaction bug since 0.6.0 (doors, the stuck item card, lifts, fog walls, graces) was one more part of the game reacting twice in that second run. That run now only draws; talk scripts, characters, game flow, events and menus run once per frame, as in the game without ERVR
- New graces work again: "Touch grace" and "Rest" (the press went nowhere, or the grace lit and you couldn't rest, and death sent you back to the old grace). NPCs and merchants run on the same scripts
- Co-op: no more crash after both players die to a boss, or when the host rests at a grace (or at the end of a session)
- Skipping a boss's phase-2 cinematic no longer leaves the view a mess (normal maps everywhere) until you die
- Launcher: unpacked into the game folder, it now says when a new ERVR is out and links the download (it can only update itself from its own folder)

0.7.4 (2026-11-06)

- Graces, plants to gather, items on the ground and dropped runes answer the button again (0.7.3 showed no prompt at a grace or plant, and item and rune prompts flickered). Boss fog walls keep working
- Lifts come when you pull their lever (the lever was pulled and the lift stayed put)

0.7.3 (2026-11-06)

- Boss fog walls work again: "Traverse the mist" shows, and the button takes you through (0.6.x, 0.7.1 and 0.7.2 never showed the prompt, and with it shown the press was thrown away). Other area prompts run by the game's event scripts are fixed the same way
- Two-handing: the hand you put on the weapon now grips the shaft instead of hovering up to 18 cm beside it

0.7.2 (2026-11-05)

- The launcher updates ERVR: when a new version is out it says so, and Update now downloads it from GitHub, checks it against the release's SHA-256 and puts it in place in a few seconds (START VR then copies it into the game, keeping your ERVR.ini). One question to GitHub at start; About > Updates turns it off
- Strikes no longer drain stamina: the game charged it inside ERVR's own attack, where the refund never looked (stamina at -50 in fights, no rolling)

0.7.1 (2026-11-05)

- Doors, levers and chests open again (0.7.0 took the press and did nothing). The ERVR.ini workaround Pass2ActionButtons=true is now built in and the line is no longer read
- A strike is one swing: it ends when the swing slows (1.5 s at the latest) and the next needs a new swing, with a short cooldown after a hit. No more constant attacking, walls hit over and over, or people hit by a sword that only touched them
- Rolls and jumps cost stamina again during a strike, and a shield guards only when you raise it in front of your chest (an arm at rest held the guard, and stamina comes back slowly while guarding)
- Flat, third person with no head, after the Grafted Scion: when the game's loading signal stays on while you walk around, ERVR treats it as stuck and comes back
- Both stick clicks together are the menu button (tap: menu, hold: map), for SteamVR, which keeps a Quest's left menu button for its dashboard
- After a death, the left stick moves the Stake of Marika / last site of grace choice
- Test switch for a lift that stays put after its lever: [VR] Pass2EventsOnce=true in ERVR.ini

0.7.0 (2026-11-04)

- ERVR Settings in the game's own menu (System, to the right of Graphics; patch 1.17.2): walking, turning, physical melee, damage, weapon weight and angle, body calibration, world scale, the HUD and rumble, changed without leaving the headset
- Body calibration: a T-pose with both triggers held measures your height and arm length; the character's arms take your length and the belt hangs at your waist. Redo it from the in-game tab or the launcher
- Turning with the stick no longer swings your weapons (it caused constant attacking and drained stamina in fights)
- The card after picking up a new item no longer stays on screen ignoring every button
- Sharper at the same GPU cost: the game renders a frame shaped like your view, 1.36x sharper on a Quest 3
- Launcher sliders save what they show (a 90 degree weapon angle was saved as 9): if you moved Smooth turn speed, Weapon angle or Body haptics in 0.6.x, set them again
- Launcher Options > Combat > Swing threshold: how fast the blade must move to strike

0.6.5 (2026-11-02)

- Doors, levers, lifts and new graces open again (0.6.0 showed the prompt and ignored Y, which kept new characters in the first room). An ERVR.ini carrying 0.6.0's value, or the 16-25 workaround, is corrected at load
- Valve Index: the left trackpad is the menu button (tap: menu, hold: map), the right trackpad's edges are the D-pad, and the grips count when squeezed instead of when touched (no more weapon wheel from resting fingers)

0.6.0 (2026-11-02) - first public release

- ERVR Launcher: unpack anywhere, press START VR. It finds the game, checks its patch, the VR runtime and the headset, installs ERVR, parks flat render mods, turns off depth of field, motion blur and ray tracing, and starts the game with EAC off or through Seamless Co-op
- Seamless Co-op: partners play the ordinary game; your hits count only your own damage; deaths, joins and leaves no longer crash or break the view
- Game patch 1.17.2 (exe 2.7.2.0) next to 1.16.2 and 1.17.1
- Weapon wheel: hold a grip with the hand still, let go over a weapon to switch, with the game's own icons
- Body haptics on bHaptics TactSuit, TactSleeves and TactVisor through the bHaptics Player
- Combat: one hit per swing scaled by its quality; swings no longer stop registering after a spent swing
- Frame rate follows the headset (no more half-rate 45 Hz under Virtual Desktop); exact 16:9 render sizes keep menus and the map on the HUD panel
- Fixes: view near the ground or stuck crouched, black headset at the title screen, a pickup dialog that ignored every button, a long-session co-op crash

0.4.0 (2026-09-10) - never published; everything in it ships in 0.6.0

- Own OpenXR session (Oculus / VDXR / SteamVR), true stereo with both eyes at the same simulation instant, cinema fallback for menus and cutscenes
- HUD lifted out of the frame onto its own floating panel at headset resolution
- First person: head-bone camera, held body pose, arms on the controllers, head hiding, Torrent riding
- Physical melee: swings fire the game's own attack event; damage, poise, status and stamina from the game; damage scales with swing speed; weapon weight and inertia

- Motion controllers mapped to XInput with tap/hold layers, haptics, stick menu navigation, no gamepad required
- Verified address maps for game exe 2.6.2.0 (1.16.2) and 2.7.1.0 (1.17.1)
- No telemetry, no network code; builds are attested by a public GitHub Actions workflow
