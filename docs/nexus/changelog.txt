0.6.5 (2026-11-02)

- Doors, levers, lifts and new graces open again (0.6.0 showed the prompt and ignored Y, which kept new characters in the first room). An ERVR.ini carrying 0.6.0's value, or the 16-25 workaround, is corrected at load
- Valve Index: the left trackpad is the menu button (tap: menu, hold: map), the right trackpad's edges are the D-pad, and the grips count when squeezed instead of when touched (no more weapon wheel from resting fingers)
- Launcher Options: Weapon angle (how the weapon sits in your hand) and Index grip squeeze
- The belt: the four pouch shortcut items hang at your waist; grab one and press X to use it
- Torrent: found again on 1.17.2, seated rider pose, smoothed gallop camera, 45-degree free look that does not steer
- A D3D11 device from an overlay or the VR runtime no longer logs that ERVR stays idle

0.6.0 (2026-11-02) - first public release

- ERVR Launcher: unpack anywhere, press START VR. It finds the game, checks its patch, the VR runtime and the headset, installs ERVR, parks flat render mods, turns off depth of field, motion blur and ray tracing, and starts the game with EAC off or through Seamless Co-op
- Seamless Co-op: partners play the ordinary game; your hits count only your own damage; deaths, joins and leaves no longer crash or break the view
- Game patch 1.17.2 (exe 2.7.2.0) next to 1.16.2 and 1.17.1
- Weapon wheel: hold a grip with the hand still, let go over a weapon to switch, with the game's own icons
- Body haptics on bHaptics TactSuit, TactSleeves and TactVisor through the bHaptics Player
- Combat: one hit per swing scaled by its quality; swings no longer stop registering after a spent swing
- Frame rate follows the headset (no more half-rate 45 Hz under Virtual Desktop); exact 16:9 render sizes keep menus and the map on the HUD panel
- Fixes: view near the ground or stuck crouched, black headset at the title screen, a pickup dialog that ignored every button, a long-session co-op crash

0.4.0 (2026-09-10) - never published; everything in it ships in 0.6.0

- Own OpenXR session (Oculus / VDXR / SteamVR), true stereo with both eyes at the same simulation instant, cinema fallback for menus and cutscenes
- HUD lifted out of the frame onto its own floating panel at headset resolution
- First person: head-bone camera, held body pose, arms on the controllers, head hiding, Torrent riding
- Physical melee: swings fire the game's own attack event; damage, poise, status and stamina from the game; damage scales with swing speed; weapon weight and inertia
- Two-hand by grabbing the hilt; Ash of War as its effect without the animation; spells and bows aim along the casting hand
- Motion controllers mapped to XInput with tap/hold layers, haptics, stick menu navigation, no gamepad required
- Verified address maps for game exe 2.6.2.0 (1.16.2) and 2.7.1.0 (1.17.1)
- No telemetry, no network code; builds are attested by a public GitHub Actions workflow
